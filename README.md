# NegosyoOS PH

> **Temporary working name.** Final branding has not been approved or screened.

NegosyoOS PH is one mobile-first, AI-assisted application for Philippine MSME owners. It combines two bounded domains inside one product:

- **Start & Comply** — business registration, permits, tax-readiness, renewals, amendments, authorised representatives, and closure.
- **Operate & Decide** — inventory or materials, customer jobs or orders, purchases, outflows, discrepancies, owner actions, and outcome verification.

## Current status

**Phase 1A — Foundation Prototype**

Coding is authorised for a narrow prototype. This is not commercial validation, final architecture approval, or selection of the first MSME vertical.

The first prototype will build:

1. shared account and business foundations;
2. a small Start & Comply case-tracking workflow based on the Pasig car-tint installation-services experience;
3. a controlled BMBE certification and status-tracking workflow; and
4. a small Operate & Decide inventory and purchasing workflow based on the Mandaluyong DUO BREW discovery case.

These reference workflows do not make Pasig the nationwide standard, do not select CaféOS as the first vertical, and do not automatically declare any business BMBE-qualified or income-tax exempt.

A separate B2B service reference case involving air-conditioning installation, cleaning, and repair for corporate and bank clients is preserved for later job-centred validation.
